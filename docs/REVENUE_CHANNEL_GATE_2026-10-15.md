## Proposed minimum paid-conversion experiment (not authorized to launch)

- Path: **one native NAVER Premium Contents individual paid product**, not another website or SEO production rollout.
- Candidate artifact: **Korean moving quote omissions/comparison checklist + spreadsheet** (a *hypothesis*, not the winner; related Naver keyword `포장이사견적비교` 6,840 monthly searches, `이사견적비교` 390, `손없는날` 90,600; latter is broader and must NOT be treated as direct purchase intent).
- A free demonstration sample must prove value in <60 seconds and disclose what file does and does not calculate, with optional paid detailed artifact. No invented vendor quotes, false savings, user phone capture, or regulated financial claims.
- Price hypothesis 9,900 or 12,900 KRW, subject to competitor/intent evidence. At **12,900 KRW**, ignoring tax/refunds/operating cost, post-platform-fee unit cash is roughly `12900*(1-0.1064)=11527.44 KRW`, so **at least 87 gross full-price sales/month** would be needed to reach 1m before taxes/other costs. Neither demand nor possibility of acquiring 87 buyers is established.
- **STOP if:** sales channel not accessible/approved, sales effort unexpectedly requires recurring manual work, no meaningful difference from free examples, platform publishing restricted, no compliant distribution method, or no real purchase response in an appropriately sized first test. A 'zero sale in 14 days' trial alone is not interpretable if near-zero visitors; track listing visits and funnel.
- Separate confirmed **verified first sale** from estimated and finally paid net revenue.
- Never auto-publish commercial content, accept terms or alter accounts/production without owner authorization; do not create/charge seller accounts on speculation.

## Highest-value remaining blockers

1. Do we have owner-authorized access to a NAVER Premium Contents **seller** account? NAVER Search Ads account is **not** the seller account. Its status is UNKNOWN.
2. No actual qualified paying customers or listing view-to-purchase conversion.
3. No validated willingness-to-pay for the exact moving spreadsheet. Examples prove paid templates can be **listed** but not that our specific product will sell.
4. No authorized low-effort distribution channel delivering actual relevant viewers; website Google indexing remains unproven.

## Decision

**TEST PLANNING ONLY** (read-only sources verified, first sellable artifact and public launch HOLD). No new Worker, WordPress updates, unattended social posting, site changes, ad purchase, private credentials, KDP account, or payment onboarding done. The next approval sought must be specific: using/create a NAVER Premium seller account and publish exactly one lawful zero-extra-cost digital item **after** product validation. If onboarding/fulfillment cannot be delegated with low labor, pivot to KDP marketplace research rather than launching another standalone site.
